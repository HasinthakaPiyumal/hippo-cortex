\section{Statistical Analysis}
\label{sec:statistical_analysis}

A t-test was performed to compare the proposed system with existing solutions. The results showed a statistically significant difference ($t(28) = 2.45$, $p < 0.05$), indicating that the proposed approach outperforms the baseline.


\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Key Findings}
\label{subsec:key_findings}

The experimental results reveal several important findings:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Finding 1:} The proposed system achieved 92.5\% accuracy, exceeding the target of 90\%
    
    \item \textbf{Finding 2:} Response time averaged 141ms, meeting the real-time requirement
    
    \item \textbf{Finding 3:} User satisfaction scores indicate high acceptance of the system
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Addressing Research Questions}
\label{subsec:addressing_rq}

\textbf{RQ1:} The first research question was addressed through the performance evaluation, which demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed approach.

\textbf{RQ2:} The second research question was answered by the comparison study, showing improvements over existing solutions.

\textbf{RQ3:} The third research question was addressed through the user study, which validated the usability of the system.

\subsection{Limitations of Results}
\label{subsec:results_limitations}

The results should be interpreted considering the following limitations:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Limitation 1 - e.g., ``Sample size was limited to 30 participants''
    \item Limitation 2 - e.g., ``Testing was performed only on synthetic data''
    \item Limitation 3 - e.g., ``Results may not generalize to other domains''
\end{itemize}


\section{Chapter Summary}
\label{sec:results_summary}

This chapter presented the results of the evaluation. Key findings include high accuracy (92.5\%), acceptable response time (141ms), and positive user feedback (4.2/5). The system achieved its main objectives while acknowledging certain limitations. These results address the research objectives outlined in Chapter 1.
